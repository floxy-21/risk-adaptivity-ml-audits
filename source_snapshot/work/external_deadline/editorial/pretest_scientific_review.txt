Core design assessment
The evaluator implements a coherent fixed-pair decision-value benchmark.
Every method starts from identical forecasts, observed targets, exact totals
and free deductions. Each actual batch is selected without hidden reference
arguments, charged in full and then revealed; no prefix refund or separately
optimized nonnested-budget shortcut is used. Queries are distinct and
eligible, the cap is enforced, and reapplication follows a complete reveal
and aggregate closure. Stopping costs include all cases and failures, rather
than only successful comparisons. The batch optimizer's width guarantee is
not misrepresented as an optimal stopping-cost theorem.

The early/later archive rules preserve every permitted saved forecast row,
including future-target forecasts. Under the declared lag2 issue schedule,
the first direct copy of dayD is dayD+2 persistence issued at the later
cutoff. Same-day target availability is separate from forecast generation.
Source-clock mapping, constructed totals and simulated availability are
explicit assumptions; no physical transmission time or selective meter
transaction saving is measured.

Hara calibration uses the full validation window. The source amendment
correctly identifies validation replay as retrospective rather than a claim
that its final SD existed at each earlier validation cutoff. Test acquisition
is after that calibration window. Household selection uses source training
quality; zero-test attrition is retained rather than replaced. The LCL source
is external to the original paper but remains a selected historical panel.

Interpretation limits to preserve after outcomes
- A cumulative budget is per daily pair and counts requested hourly target
  records; in LCL each target sums two half-hour source rows. It is not a
  measured meter transaction, network request or shared all-model cost.
- B80 is an integer threshold of a household-macro conditional curve.
  Households with no initially unresolved cases have undefined conditional
  ratios; report their exclusions. Ties remain in denominators. Do not
  interpolate fractional budgets or use a favorable subset of model pairs.
- Mean query cost includes failures. Strict certification and numerical
  tie stopping are different statuses; exact reference ties are separately
  recorded. A numerical tie is not asserted to be an exact mathematical tie.
- Hara/uniform/randomized results are three seeded executable paths per
  specified day/pair mask design, not exact expected stopping costs.
- Independent interval replay checks outcomes and accounting, but correct
  certificates under feasible totals are not themselves a new measured
  predictive-accuracy superiority claim.
- Selector target blindness is an audited API/code information-flow claim.
  The evaluator process contains reference targets for masking, totals,
  reveals and validation; it is not a physically isolated secret-data system.
