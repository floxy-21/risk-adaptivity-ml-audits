EXTERNAL FIXED-DEADLINE AUDIT — PRE-OUTCOME ANALYSIS PROTOCOL
Written 11 October 2026, before model fitting or acquisition outcomes.

Question: how many distinct paid readings does a feasible sequential policy
request to certify a fixed daily model comparison at an early audit, and
how does this change when later issued forecasts copy those targets?
This is a controlled benchmark with constructed temporal totals and a
declared UTC issue schedule. It measures neither transmission latency nor
money saved nor availability of selective retrieval in a deployed utility.

SOURCE AND DATES
Independent source: official REFIT raw-release whole-house Aggregate power,
all 20 candidate households (IDs 1–21 except14), subject to source-only
inventory and frozen source protocol/amendments. Source source_protocol.txt
and source amendment distinguish event-change intermediate filling and
Part1 zero-coded missing values. Prefer Part2 only; do not use filled cleaned
records as unqualified measured truth. Targets are quality-filtered
zero-order-hold hourly energy references, not exact physical integrations.
Tentative periods: Jan–Sep2014 train, Oct–Dec2014 validation, Jan–Mar2015 test.
Source-only date/eligibility feasibility may be amended BEFORE fitting or
acquisition, with reason, previous rule and full household inventory kept.
No result-based changes to dates, masks, households, seeds or coverage target.
All source-eligible households and all feature/reference-eligible test days
are included. At least30 eligible training days are needed to fit models.

FORECASTS AND INFORMATION
Four fixed families: persistence, Ridge, histogram gradient boosting, MLP.
Retain existing study hyperparameters and seed1729; no test tuning, retries,
architecture search or use of old fitted models. For target UTC dayD issue
at noonD-1 using hourly values of D-2,D-3,D-8, meanD-2, known hour/day-of-week/
month cyclic variables. Complete input dayD-2 ends at startD-1, before issue.
Fit parameters on training only and keep fixed during validation and test.
Predictions clipped nonnegative, MLP targets normalized by training mean.
Generate a NEW archive including future target days through test_end+2.
Persistence is issued whenever its D-2 source is present, even if learned
feature rows are unavailable. Learned forecasts require complete features,
not the future reference target. Preserve all available forecasts per cutoff.
Early cutoff=00:00D+1. Later cutoff=noonD+1 after forecast issue. No direct
copy of D can be available early; D+2 persistence issued later copies D.
Archive provenance records issue, target and source timestamps. Hidden
reference data are separate from policy-selection inputs. Free copies must
come from permitted archive rows, then singleton and zero-total deductions.

MASKS AND PAIRS
All six unordered named model pairs. One contiguous six-hour gap per day
and seed; start uniformly over integers0..18. Mask seeds1103,2207,3301,
stable SHA256-derived per-house/day streams, identical across policies.
Four constructed exact six-hour totals: hours0–5,6–11,12–17,18–23.
All24 scheduled hours enter the daily MSE contrast. Masks affect evaluation
targets, not historically available prediction features or model training.

ACQUISITION AND STOPPING
Policies: exact deterministic, greedy, Hara acquisition + aggregate
certificate, uniform without replacement, exact randomized with the
predeclared uniform-barycenter secondary objective. Each policy uses the
same observations, forecasts, totals and reveal access. Hara uses Gaussian
mean from fixed Ridge and household validation RMSE as SD, floored at
1e-8 times training mean; condition its proposal on residual aggregate sums.
Gaussian support may include negative values: this is an approximate
sampling proposal, not the certificate's feasible set or a fitted truth law.
Use published squared-loss expected absolute contrast, not |a-b| alone.

At each stage apply all free deductions; compute sharp bounds; stop when
strict ordering is certified or width is numerically zero. Otherwise select
up to2 eligible distinct coordinates, capped by remaining total budget6;
charge the full batch before revealing all its entries. Repeat with residual
constraints. No refund for a certificate that a batch prefix would have
provided. Never count separately optimized nonnested budgets as a trajectory.
The sequence is a myopic application of the exact batch-width optimizer,
not a globally optimal strategy for minimum reads-to-decision. Budgets and
costs are per day/model pair, not shared across all candidate models.
Acquisition's documented lexicographic flat-objective refinement prevents
an empty deterministic batch from making a positive-width trajectory stall.
Randomized/Hara/uniform policies use one draw stream per mask seed, identified
by household/day/pair/policy/seed/stage. These are three finite seeded paths,
not exact expected stopping costs or selection of the best random outcome.

OUTCOMES AND PRACTICAL CRITERION
Report strict certification curves by integer cumulative paid readings0..6:
(a) all eligible comparisons;(b) initially unresolved comparisons. Exact
reference ties, numerical near ties, and unresolved-at-cap cases stay in the
denominators and are reported separately. Do not call a near tie exact.
Report all-case and initially unresolved mean/median requested readings,
cap failures, incorrect strict certificates, enclosure violations, planning
time and household/model-pair/month summaries. Failure costs are included.
Primary curve: equal-weight mean of household initially-unresolved curves;
households with zero initial unresolved cases are explicitly counted and
excluded only from this undefined conditional ratio. Pooled curves secondary.
B80=min integerB with primary curve>=0.80, otherwise 'not reached'. Fixed80%
is checked on validation as a feasibility diagnostic; do not change it.
Practical planning target: >=15% lower B80 for deterministic exact compared
with the smallest B80 among greedy and Hara, without lower cap coverage.
Uniform is also shown, with any stronger uniform result explicitly noted.
If strongest baseline or exact does not reach80%, report that fact rather
than an undefined numeric saving. Report learned-versus-learned and household
results; do not declare broad useful gain from one favorable pair or house.
No fractional interpolation of a discrete budget or undisclosed test subset.
Bootstrap, if reported, resamples whole households with all days/seeds/pairs,
and is descriptive; shared calendar effects limit independent-house claims.

VALIDATION
Preserve protocol/source/code hashes before evaluation. Independent checker
must reconstruct every case's free copies, source timestamps, totals, bounds,
truth containment, decisions, complete-batch charges, query uniqueness,
eligibility and cap, exact test grid counts and headline aggregation without
importing acquisition code. Use scale-aware declared numerical tolerances;
floating-point tests are not a formal interval-arithmetic proof. Preserve
unfavorable and failed outcomes, and errors plus corrected rerun provenance.

The15% criterion is a preregistered-in-this-workflow planning threshold,
not a journal criterion or a public prospective preregistration. No test
outcome supports an editorial acceptance probability in advance.
