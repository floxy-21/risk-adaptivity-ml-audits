CLAIM-TO-EVIDENCE MAP

1. Sharp paired squared-loss interval under nonnegative disjoint totals.
Evidence: main mathematical result and explicit attaining completions;
independent per-stage interval/reference checks; synthetic bounds fixtures.
Route: --small; default external trajectory replay; earlier historical
checkers for the retained historical experiments.
Boundary: conditional on the declared feasible set containing the truth;
ordinary floating-point checks are not interval-arithmetic formal proofs.

2. Exact deterministic width-optimal recovery batch.
Evidence: sorted-window/allocation argument and enumerated small checks.
Route: --small and acquisition/check_acquisition.py.
Boundary: repeatedly reoptimizing such a batch does not prove optimality
for total readings required to reach a certificate. The external study
measures the resulting executable sequence empirically.

3. Exact randomized robust optimization with a specified secondary rule.
Evidence: endpoint oracle/compact formulation, hard-budget sampler,
compact-versus-finite examples and two secondary-policy examples.
Route: --small; separate prior randomized/tie/scaling checkers.
Boundary: robust expected-width optimality does not imply best realized
width, certification probability or a unique optimal distribution.

4. Qualified one-block randomization-gap bound.
Evidence: proof and attaining separated-cluster constructions, retaining
the stated positive-optimum and retained-cardinality hypotheses.
Route: earlier randomized small-instance verification, preserved in the
full historical reproduction route.
Boundary: not a statement about decision-cost gains in all measured records.

5. Certified multi-model MSE-regret stopping.
Evidence: pairwise-regret identity in the original appendix and its promoted
main-text corollary; feasible-set monotonicity proof.
Boundary: zero regret certifies best-or-tied-best; strict winner needs strict
pairwise upper bounds. A predeclared positive tolerance is measured in MSE
units. No new jointly optimal multi-model acquisition policy is claimed.

6. External early-audit certification versus requested readings.
Evidence: test/trajectories.jsonl, cases.csv, archive_snapshots.json,
coverage_curves.csv, cost_summary.csv, household_curves.csv, pair_curves.csv,
month_curves.csv and headline_summary.json, independently reconstructed
from separate reference targets and permitted forecast rows.
Route: default replay; --refit for a new fit/acquisition execution; --raw for
full official source regeneration before fresh fit/acquisition.
Boundary: the observed B80 comparison and fixed 15% planning criterion are
reported whether favorable or unfavorable. They are not an acceptance
threshold, monetary saving or globally optimal stopping theorem.

7. Early/later information contrast.
Evidence: every snapshot's available-row counts and copy-source timestamps;
new causal features and archive, including future-target predictions issued
by the cutoff. Late target copies are obtained from forecast rows, not
silently read from the hidden reference by the policy.
Route: default independent replay and forecast chronology fixtures.
Boundary: source clock and availability are declared benchmark rules;
production transmission timestamps and latency are unmeasured.

8. Faithful, explicitly adapted modern acquisition comparator.
Evidence: Hara expected-absolute squared-loss contrast, Gaussian surrogate
calibrated before test, analytical-vs-numerical integration fixtures and
common aggregate-certificate evaluation. Author-code reference and license
are retained in acquisition/.
Route: --small for formula fixtures; default for resulting traces; --refit
for newly executed acquisition.
Boundary: this is Hara acquisition plus an aggregate certificate, not a
reproduction of the original statistical estimation framework.

9. Source integrity and training-only external cohort choice.
Evidence: selected literal half-hour rows, full candidate quality inventory,
selection manifest and Decimal-based independent hourly sum checks.
Route: default bundled-source verification; --raw additionally re-extracts
cohort/reference records by reparsing the full official CSV after hash checks.
Boundary: bundled replay is not independent acquisition of the complete
source, physical clock verification, or certification that all publisher
readings were actual rather than estimated.

10. Adverse realized randomized tradeoff and computational scaling.
Evidence: original/additional-consumer frozen-policy replay; 28 predeclared
scaling cases with measured runtime/memory and eligible finite comparisons.
Route: separate unchanged historical reproduction commands, with hashes
carried forward into this revision. The new default does not rerun that
entire long suite and does not claim fresh scaling timings.
Boundary: the unfavorable measured outcomes remain. One-run scaling
measurements and variable counts do not establish runtime dominance.

11. Exact final paper source reconstruction.
Evidence: preserved pre-external source, verified external fragments, pure
editorial integration helper, frozen results and final reconstruction hash.
Route: default wrapper invokes work/build_external_manuscript.py after
checking and restoring pinned report bytes in its disposable workspace.
Boundary: this checks exact TeX bytes. PDF compilation/visual QA and journal
submission declarations are distinct checks, not consequences of a hash.
