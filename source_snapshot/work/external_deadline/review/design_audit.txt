Independent design audit before external outcomes
Reviewer scope: experiment protocol, not forecast architecture selection

The proposed early/later benchmark can close the information-timing objection if it is implemented as a newly generated archive. It cannot establish measured production latency or that a separately retained temporal register survives a real outage. REFIT whole-house Aggregate is not itself such a temporal register.

Required frozen design
1. Source and eligibility. Choose the source version, files, quality filters, conversion rule, periods and minimum eligible-day counts from a source-only inventory. Record every eligible and excluded household/date and reason. Do not inspect model rankings, recovery outcomes or policy gains before freezing this manifest. If raw REFIT Aggregate is available, preserve its missingness and sampling support. Official cleaned REFIT metadata say NaNs were forward-filled for gaps under two minutes and zeroed for longer gaps. Continuity in those cleaned timestamps alone cannot identify the filled observations. Do not call cleaned-derived targets unconditionally measured ground truth. A raw-based rule can reject hours touched by invalid/missing samples or long intervals, with a predeclared left-hold integration bound. Use UTC to retain 24-hour days, explicitly a benchmark time scale rather than local household noon. If local civil days are used instead, reject/report or explicitly weight 23/25-hour DST days.
2. Temporal information. For day D, forecasts are issued at noon D-1 using completed day D-2 and older measurements. Early audit is 00:00 D+1. It receives all saved forecasts with issue timestamp at or before that cutoff, including future-target day D+1 forecasts issued noon D. Those inputs end with D-1, so no contemporaneous D target can enter the learned forecast or persistence copy. Late audit is noon D+1 after day D+2 forecasts, whose persistence column copies D. Recompute all four models/features under this timing. Never relabel old forecasts. Define equality at cutoff and duplicate-record resolution. Produce per-row issue/target/source timestamps and archived copy trace.
3. Reference and benchmark mechanics. Fixed model predictions, the same observed coordinates, exact constructed totals, calendar and previously revealed targets go to every acquisition policy. The full target array resides in a separate reveal/evaluation component. Selection receives NaNs at unresolved coordinates, residual totals and available forecasts only. Deterministic total closure occurs before selection and after each reveal: a singleton residual is known freely, and a zero residual fixes all nonnegative entries to zero. Never query these entries.
4. Executable accounting. Begin with zero-query certification. For unresolved cases request at most two distinct eligible coordinates, also limited by remaining cap. Charge the entire batch, reveal all its readings, close aggregate deductions, recalculate bounds and then stop or continue. No within-batch refund. No requested coordinate can recur. Reoptimization is a myopic sequence of exact width-optimal batches, not a theorem of optimal decision-query cost. Distinct pair audits do not share targets unless a separate explicitly defined shared-audit policy is implemented; pair-specific cost must remain labeled.
5. Common cases and randomization. Freeze all six named pairs, all masks and seeds and the maximum paid cap; retain ties and every prescribed case. Use reproducible random streams derived from case, policy, stage and replicate identifiers; do not choose the luckiest realization. A fixed finite number of policy replicates estimates stochastic utility. Report it as Monte Carlo with its replication count, not exact expectation unless all branch probabilities have actually been integrated. The randomized LP's specified secondary criterion must depend only on current observed information, not unrevealed reference targets.
6. Main practical criterion. Freeze C(B) at each cumulative paid budget B among initially unresolved cases and also report all-case coverage. Household-macro averages avoid large households dominating. Define B80 as the smallest integer budget with C(B)>=0.80, or 'not reached' if absent within the cap. Practical success is >=15% relative reduction in B80 versus the strongest non-exact baseline (smallest B80 among greedy and the properly adapted modern acquisition method; uniform is secondary), with no inferior cap coverage and a useful distribution across households and learned-model pairs. This is a planning target, not a significance or editorial-acceptance threshold. Keep all curves even if threshold infeasible. Avoid adjusting the threshold after test inspection.
7. Further summaries. Mean, median and distribution of actually paid requests must include failures at their spent cap, and be accompanied by unresolved/tie rates. A low mean can indicate failed early stopping, so never interpret it alone as superiority. Report all-case and initially unresolved denominators. Explain that a first two-query batch can only stop at cost 2, making attainable B80 values discrete; do not interpolate a fractional query count and claim an executable advantage. Provide learned-versus-learned and household summaries before claiming general benefit. Describe household/bootstrap intervals as descriptive same-study uncertainty; common calendar/weather effects invalidate treating every pair/day as independent. Do not select a favorable pair, seed, household or time block for the headline.
8. Checks. Reconstruct bounds from recorded observed values/totals and saved forecasts independently; verify reference enclosure, zero incorrect strict certificates, no forecast/feature timestamp violations, source-trace identity, monotone shrinking intervals after information, query eligibility/uniqueness/caps, full-batch charges, common case counts, and exact agreement of raw tables with manuscript summaries. Normalize numerical tolerances consistently, preserve near ties as a separate tolerance band, and do not call floating-point tests a formal arithmetic certificate.
9. Runtime. State whether planning time includes feature/surrogate fitting, LP setup, LP solve and posterior closure; report at least policy selection wall time separately from common fitting. Compare overhead with readings saved as two measured quantities; there are no observed monetary or transmission savings. Later zero-query reconstruction supplies a benchmark wait-versus-query comparison, not measured device recovery latency.
