LOW CARBON LONDON SOURCE FALLBACK PROTOCOL
Frozen 2026-10-11 before LCL values, quality counts, model fits or acquisition outcomes were inspected.

Reason for source change
The REFIT raw source inventory under the frozen quality rule yields no complete days in the households processed so far. REFIT is retained as a failed source-feasibility attempt; its rule is not loosened to produce results. No REFIT forecasting or recovery outcome was evaluated. Low Carbon London provides direct half-hour energy readings and explicit missing values, removing the need to integrate event-expanded power. Adoption remains conditional on verifying the official source documentation and quality inventory.

Sampling fixed before inspection
Collect the100 lowest distinct household IDs in numeric/lexicographic source-ID order. This is a deterministic bounded source panel, not a representative population sample.
Within those100, retain the20 lowest IDs with at least90 complete training days after the source screening below. Household selection uses training-period quality only. No validation/test counts, energy magnitudes, forecast accuracy, model rankings or acquisition outcomes affect selection.
If fewer than20 satisfy the criterion, retain all qualifying IDs and report the shortfall rather than searching later IDs.
The selected IDs are frozen before model fitting. Every subsequent available validation/test day is included subject only to the unchanged reference and causal-lag availability rules.

Chronology
Training: 2013-01-01 inclusive to2013-10-01 exclusive.
Validation: 2013-10-01 inclusive to2014-01-01 exclusive.
Test: 2014-01-01 inclusive to2014-03-01 exclusive.
Preserve at least8 previous days for causal lag features and2 later days where records exist.
Timestamp interpretation must follow source documentation. If the documentation does not establish a physical timezone, retain the database-clock convention and explicitly disclose that benchmark deadlines are constructed; do not invent a UTC assertion. Duplicate or missing clock entries are excluded as below.

Reference and screening
Each eligible hour requires exactly2 distinct, finite, nonnegative kWh readings on its :00 and :30 source-clock positions. Sum those2 directly; no interpolation, zero replacement, partial-hour rescaling or power integration.
Null/nonfinite/negative readings and duplicate timestamp groups are flagged and invalidate the hour. Do not silently merge even equal duplicates.
Each complete day requires all24 eligible hourly references. Invalid/unavailable hours carry no energy reference value. Record exclusions by household/date/reason.
If an original quality flag marks an estimated/substituted observation, exclude it. If no such flag is provided, say so; finite values do not establish the absence of every source-side estimation practice.
Tariff status is retained for the inventory but does not determine inclusion or exclusion.
Constructed temporal totals are sums of the eligible hourly references and are controlled benchmark inputs, not independent outage-surviving registers.
