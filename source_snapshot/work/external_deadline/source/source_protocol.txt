TIME AND REFERENCE DEFINITION
Use source Unix timestamps in UTC when present. UTC 24-hour days avoid local DST ambiguity.
Training: 2014-01-01 inclusive to 2014-10-01 exclusive.
Validation: 2014-10-01 inclusive to 2015-01-01 exclusive.
Test: 2015-01-01 inclusive to 2015-04-01 exclusive.
Preserve at least eight earlier days to construct causal lag features, where source data permit.
Between successive raw observations at times t_i < t_(i+1), define power by zero-order hold at the value p_i.
The hourly reference is the resulting integral divided by 3,600,000, in kWh. It is a reference based on sampled power, not exact physical energy.
An interval is admissible only if its duration is positive and <=16 seconds and its power is finite and nonnegative. Do not interpolate across longer gaps, fill missing power, clip high power, or replace invalid entries.
Any nonfinite or negative reading invalidates its supported interval. Any duplicate-timestamp group is quarantined (even equal readings) and both its adjacent integration intervals are invalidated. Backward timestamps are recorded; any sorting needed is explicitly recorded and all directly implicated timestamps and adjacent intervals are quarantined. Unknown source parsing or provenance issues stop preparation rather than silently changing this rule.
An hour is eligible only if all 3,600 seconds have admissible support with no quarantined intervals. No partial-hour scaling or imputation.
A day is source-complete only if all 24 hours are eligible.
Constructed temporal totals are sums of these reference hours and must be described as benchmark inputs.

HOUSEHOLD ELIGIBILITY
Require >=90 source-complete training days, >=20 validation days and >=20 test days, before lag-feature availability is considered.
Root forecasting protocol additionally requires >=30 eligible training days after causal lag features are available and reports those additional exclusions.
Every household satisfying these fixed conditions is retained.
Publish per-household/date coverage and exclusions. Exclusion reasons and source row counts are retained even when a household is ineligible.
No forecasting errors, model rankings, recovery benefits, or acquisition decisions are computed during source preparation.

PROVENANCE
Record original URLs, retrieval status, bytes and SHA-256 for retrieved inputs; archive member inventory; exact parser source hash; UTC run timestamp; output hashes; schema; and license attribution.
If the raw source is inaccessible or the rule leaves insufficient reference records, report that concrete feasibility problem before changing source or protocol.
