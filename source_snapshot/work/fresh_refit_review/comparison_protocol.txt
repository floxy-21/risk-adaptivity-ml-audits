FRESH REFIT: INDEPENDENT COMPARISON PROTOCOL
Frozen 2026-10-11 UTC before fresh fitting outcomes are available.

Scope
Compare one fresh isolated forecasts-only rerun of all original 34 series and
all 30 previously unused Portugal consumers against their original saved
predictions and per-fit metadata. No new model, hyperparameter, consumer,
train/test split, mask, or recovery policy is selected. Do not rerun the full
mask grid merely to compare fitted forecasts. Original files remain immutable.

Inputs and identity
Reference prediction files are outputs/study_predictions.csv and
outputs/study_holdout_consumers_predictions.csv. Reference fitting metadata
are the corresponding study_summary.json and study_holdout_consumers.json.
Fresh files are fresh_predictions.csv, fit_metadata.json and fresh_result.json
under this directory. Compare exact cohort/meter/timestamp keys, uniqueness,
row counts, all four model columns, truth, and meter/cohort membership. Preserve
original cohort labels and timezone representations. Expected total is 64
distinct series and 166,536 hourly rows (6,939 meter-days).

Predeclared numerical criteria
For a numeric scalar x and its frozen reference r, closeness means
abs(x-r) <= absolute_tolerance + relative_tolerance*abs(r).
- Truth and daily-persistence values: absolute=1e-12, relative=1e-12.
- Ridge, histogram gradient boosting and MLP predictions: absolute=1e-8,
  relative=1e-8, on the original kWh scale after prescribed nonnegative clipping.
- Independently recomputed MSE, RMSE and MAE, and saved complete-reference MSE:
  absolute=1e-8, relative=1e-8.
- Training mean and MLP target scale: absolute=1e-12, relative=1e-12.
- MLP final training objective loss: absolute=1e-8, relative=1e-8.
- Model configuration, seeds, training/test calendar endpoints and day counts,
  MLP epochs and captured warning strings: exact equality.
Record exact numeric equality as well as maximum absolute/relative/scaled
discrepancies; do not demand serialized byte identity. Floating reductions may
differ even for a faithful run. Any tolerance exceedance is reported as a
failure requiring diagnosis, never remedied by tuning or loosening this file.
Any convergence warning, nonfinite value, negative clipped forecast, missing
row, consumer substitution, changed split or configuration must be reported.
Also compare complete-period model rankings using the manuscript's 1e-9
scale-dependent tie convention; report any change even when forecast-level
differences pass. Counts describe this rerun, not independent statistical data.

Independent checks
The checker must not call fit_forecasts or import the new refit driver to
produce its comparison numbers. Recompute residual MSE/RMSE/MAE directly from
fresh and frozen hourly rows, and reconcile stored per-fit MSE. Inspect the
unchanged trainer's explicit feature/split/scaling rules and fresh driver's
cache handling. Where feasible, independently reconstruct causal feature
fixtures, DST exclusions, and train/test chronology from declared raw hourly
inputs, without fitting any second model. Declare fixture scope precisely.

Raw/chronology rules to preserve
Household: all 60 valid minute values per hour; sum/60, no partial-hour filling.
Portugal: shift quarter-hour endpoint timestamps back 15 minutes, then sum
four readings/4; original first 30 training-eligible meters among first 80,
additional first 30 eligible among MT_081..MT_370. Eligibility uses only 2013
availability >=99% and positivity >95%. No replacement after test outcomes.
BLEM: integer counter subtraction at exact UTC hour boundaries before unit
conversion by 1e10; all 3-minute interior snapshots present and nondecreasing.
Do not read old hourly pickle caches. Rebuild them only in isolated workspace.
Retain original BLEM CSV round trip before original training to preserve
declared arithmetic. Feature lags 1/2/7 days and previous-day mean must precede
the target day's simulated issue time; calendar features are deterministic.
Provider-local EU DST affected days and lag-dependent days are excluded for
household/Portugal; BLEM uses UTC. The original complete-day/history eligibility
is retained. Training periods: household 2008-2009, Portugal 2013, BLEM before
2017-10-01; testing: household 2010, Portugal first 28 days of Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct
2014, BLEM from 2017-10-01. Use the original fixed seeds and two-thread fit limit.

Limits
This is a fresh execution of the original trainer from pinned raw sources,
with an independent arithmetic/comparison checker. It is not an independent
reimplementation of Ridge/HGB/MLP training, an external-cohort validation,
new hypothesis confirmation, or a rerun of all downstream masks and optimizers.
Local protocol time and hashes are provenance, not external preregistration.
