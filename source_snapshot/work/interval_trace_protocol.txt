Frozen interval-total recovery sensitivity protocol
Created 2026-10-10 UTC before new sensitivity outcomes were evaluated.

Population: all original 34 series and all 3,729 saved held-out meter-days in
outputs/study_predictions.csv. No new training, source changes, consumer selection,
model selection, tuning, fit retries, or outcome-dependent exclusions.
Four original forecasts, all six unordered pairs, seeds 1103/2207/3301.
Exactly the original six-hour contiguous masks (gap index 1) and six-hour blocks.
Total intervals: [(1-tau)T,(1+tau)T], tau=0,0.01,0.05, with T computed before masking
from the original complete block. These are constructed sensitivity intervals,
not calibrated instrument error, independently measured backups, or random noise.

Two evaluator regimes: restricted_record_closed and saved_forecast_archive_closed.
The latter uses every available saved test persistence copy exactly as in
study_archive_access.py, via its forecast-only timestamp/daily interface, with no
truth fallback. Both regimes include all exact deductions: zero upper residual
fixes every unknown to zero; an exact residual total fixes a lone unknown. A
nonzero-width interval does not fix a singleton. Subtract all observed/free values
from the lower and upper block totals; truncate the residual lower at zero.
Roundoff convention: infeasible discrepancies within 1e-9*max(1,abs(total bounds))
may be clipped to zero, as in the original exact-total implementation; this is
not a physical tolerance. No positive uncertain interval is collapsed as exact.

Evaluate budget zero and at most two exact target queries per day/model pair.
Exact policy: unchanged interval_total_recovery.optimal_queries.
Comparator: deterministic one-step minimization of the same worst remaining
interval width; lowest target index breaks ties. Continue while a remaining
block has positive objective width and budget remains, including zero immediate
gain steps. Full-block recovery is allowed; no observed, freely reconstructed,
or exactly deduced coordinate may be queried. Batch choices receive no hidden
target values. Apply queries and only then use their actual saved target answers.
Already certified cases remain included, matching the original fixed-budget design.

Report all cohorts, both access regimes, every tolerance and policy: certification,
wrong decisions, truth enclosure, mean paid queries, raw and training-mean-squared
normalized initial/actual/worst widths, actual/worst fraction of initial width,
exact-minus-greedy coverage and width changes, free and exact-deduction counts.
Average observations within meter, then average meters equally within cohort;
report pooled counts separately. Query/policy repeats are dependent evaluations.
Use original decision margin 1e-9*max(1,abs(lower),abs(upper)).

Validate integration with independent linear-program endpoints on deterministic
small fixtures, explicit singleton/zero-energy cases, hidden-answer invariance,
and all-case enclosure and actual-width<=predicted-worst checks. At tau=0 compare
initial bounds and optimal objective against original exact-total implementation;
record query/decision differences if ties or explicit closure alter bookkeeping.
Also reconcile aggregate tau=0 rates and widths against the saved archive study;
report deviations honestly rather than require identical optimizer ties.

No instrument-performance, retrieval-cost, latency, field-deployment, or independent
population generalization claim. Aggregate CSVs only: do not redistribute BLEM
hourly targets or forecast copies. Hash all source inputs before and after; no
existing artifacts may change. At most two parallel workers; first completed
meter's progress provides timing without changing study design. All cases run.
