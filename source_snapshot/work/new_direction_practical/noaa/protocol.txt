Frozen bounded observational feasibility protocol, 2026-10-10
Internal research; not a manuscript claim or a deployment study.

This protocol is saved before decoding any GEFS forecast values. The observational screen inspected the first three lexical CONUS COOP HPD station CSVs, finding nine post-2000 simple a/A accumulated days at USC00010140 (Alberta, Alabama). Selection is therefore purposive, source-based, and not a representative prevalence estimate. All nine days are retained regardless of forecast outcome: 2008-12-05,10,11,17,28,29 and 2009-06-05,14,24. All other calendar days in December 2008 and June 2009 are controls if every hourly record is present, nonnegative, and has blank quality flag and no accumulation marker. Report every excluded day/reason. No model-based event replacement or tuning.

Observation source: NOAA COOP Hourly Precipitation Data v2 station CSV, pinned by SHA256. Current v2 documentation defines HR00 as 00:00-01:00 local standard time and HR23 as 23:00-24:00. Station inventory offset is UTC-6. A complete a at00, dots01-22, A at23 is a24-hour sum, not a23-hour sum. Values are hundredths of an inch; convert to mm with0.254. Only the original A-marked recorded total is the natural coarse constraint. DlySum is a calculated partial sum and is never treated as an independent backup measurement. Retain quality/source fields verbatim. Inspect documented public15-minute/raw alternate records and apply any target-identifying information found before reporting natural uncertainty. A failed endpoint is an access limitation, not proof of nonexistence.

Forecasts: NOAA GEFSv12 retrospective reforecast, 00UTC issue on each target local calendar day, fixed c00,p01,p02,p03,p04 members. Compare c00 with the arithmetic mean of all five members, no member selection. These are retrospective hindcasts, not archived forecasts actually issued in2008/2009, and share model physics/initialization; no ML or SOTA claim. Evaluate nearest public0.25-degree grid point to station32.2322,-87.4104, with grid/station mismatch explicit. Forecasts for eight three-hour bins cover lead6-30h, corresponding to local00-24. GEFS APCP metadata alternate3h and6h accumulations; derive disjoint3h increments by differencing nested6h fields with their3h predecessor. Verify initialization, member, units, start/end steps, station gridpoint, and nonnegative derived values; fail rather than silently clip material negatives.

Data acquisition: public NOAA S3 index plus byte-range requests for messages ending9,12,15,18,21,24,27,30h. Record URL, index SHA, byte range, returned Content-Range/ETag, range SHA, size, and decoded metadata. No full-object SHA claim for a byte range. Missing forecast download or decoding failures remain reported exclusions, not replacement dates.

Primary analysis is zero-query paired MSE contrast (control minus ensemble mean), eight equally weighted3h targets/day. For natural accumulated days y>=0,sum y=T gives sharp endpoints C+T*min(c),C+T*max(c), C=mean(a^2-b^2),c=2(b-a)/8. All accessible finer observations constrain the same set. Keep every day; report endpoint signs/width, total forecast biases and a uniform-allocation point as a non-certified comparator. Do not claim correctness of a point decision where finer truth is absent. Complete controls use their observed8bin targets to check interval enclosure/endpoints under an explicitly labelled artificial coarsening diagnostic, separate from natural cases. Also report direct observed MSE for complete controls.

Whole-panel endpoint sums use independent daily recorded constraints; no forecast artifact known here copies the future gauge labels, unlike the former persistence experiment. Exactness is conditional on reported-record totals and nonnegative bins, not a claim of exact atmospheric precipitation measurement. Documented gauge precision/accuracy and spatial representativeness limit physical interpretation. Do not manufacture an error distribution or calibrate tolerance from outcomes. No paid recovery, acquisition cost, recoverability of old paper traces, field deployment, or acceptance-probability claim.

Finite success criterion: successfully produce and validate provenance-linked natural cases that remain genuinely temporally unresolved after the documented public archive check, plus fixed forecast comparisons and clearly scoped controls. This demonstrates a natural application of zero-query set-valued evaluation, not practical superiority or general frequency. If all natural cases are resolved by accessible finer records, or no decisions become informative, retain and report that negative result. No additional stations/months/models selected after outcomes.
