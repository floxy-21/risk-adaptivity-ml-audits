This post-review analysis addresses evaluator-information consistency on the
original frozen 34-series study. It does not refit models, select consumers,

All original source scripts, forecasts, summaries and results remain unchanged.

REGIMES AND THEIR INFORMATION
restricted_daily_product_closed: isolated daily evaluators each receive only
that day's fixed forecasts, surviving targets and retained aggregate bands.
They perform direct exact-aggregate deductions. A summary-only period aggregator
receives their scalar comparison bounds and point contrasts, not the cross-day
raw forecast arrays. This is an explicitly restricted historical reference;
it is NOT a closed information model for an analyst holding all period vectors.
saved_forecast_archive_closed: the evaluator receives every saved TEST forecast
row for the same meter, for all four candidates, including later rows lying
inside the evaluated period. A documented daily-persistence forecast at t+24
is the true target at t in this fixed causal pipeline. Incorporate EVERY such
available direct copy, then exact-aggregate deductions, before any comparison.
Within-period raw-vector access and complete saved-test-archive access coincide
here; no outside-period forecast or arrival-time assumption is introduced.
Both regimes use a declared product feasible set after these deductions, not
an assertion of nonlinear inversion/full informational closure. Nonlinear
inversion, retained feature caches, unsaved forecasts and raw-target fallback
are excluded. Training targets used for shape/donor baselines are the original
chronological training data, not additional held-out observations.

ORIGINAL GRID AND SHARED INFORMATION

mask, seed1103/2207/3301, block6/24 and relative total tolerance0/.01/.05.
High-demand masks remain the original oracle stress with repeated seeds, not

Timestamp+daily-forecast columns alone reconstruct copied values; validate

Uncertain positive-total singleton blocks remain uncertain. Every comparator

WHOLE-PERIOD EVALUATION
Sum the per-day sharp paired and separate individual-loss bounds, then divide
by the number of eligible days: every day has24 targets, so this is the fixed
full-period mean with denominator24*days. No extra period-wide query budget is
introduced. Four point evaluators are reported and matched in coverage for
EXACT totals only: pooled complete-case contrast, uniform completion,
weekday/hour profile, and historical training-day donor. BEFORE execution,
the denominator convention was clarified: new complete-case scores pool the
observed squared-loss sums over all days and divide by the total observed count,
which can change after reconstruction. Traces retain both numerator and count.
The legacy equal-day mean of observed-only scores remains a compatibility
diagnostic, not the newly reported whole-period complete-case score. Other point
scores use all24 hourly targets per day.
Profiles and donor candidates come only from the original eligible training
days, with original DST/history filtering and no model fits. Donor selection
uses the enlarged observed targets, never hidden truth or candidate forecasts.
