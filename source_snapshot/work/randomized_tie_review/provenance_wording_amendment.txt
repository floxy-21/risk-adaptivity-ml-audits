PROVENANCE WORDING CLARIFICATION - 11 October 2026 UTC

Independent source-order checking identified an overly literal sentence in
the frozen protocol: audit_ties.py reads file bytes to compute the SHA256 of
review_frozen_forecasts.csv before selecting policies. It does not parse or
use those reference target values in either optimization stage. The policies
are saved and hashed before pandas reads the CSV for outcome evaluation.

Accordingly, "not read" in the protocol means "not parsed or used for policy
selection," not "no filesystem bytes accessed for hashing." The original
protocol, script, results and hashes remain intact as historical records.
This clarification changes neither policy inputs nor any experimental result.
