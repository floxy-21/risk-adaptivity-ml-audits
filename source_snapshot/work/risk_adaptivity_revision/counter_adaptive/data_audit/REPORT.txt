HOURLY TARGETS AND RETAINED TOTALS
For exact UTC endpoints, y_h=[C(h+1)-C(h)]/10^10 kWh. Subtract Python integers
before floating conversion; never subtract two rounded large binary64 counters.
Existing preparation: work/data_prepare_blem.py. It additionally requires all
21 fine-grid snapshots from h through h+1 and no decrease among the20 increments.
No interpolation, nearest-neighbor matching, rescaling or reset correction is
used. A decrease or missing interior reading excludes the hour; no such exclusion
occurs in these three source files. Day/lag completeness then excludes the first
partial day, final partial day, and the first seven complete days needed for
lagged features. No empirical observed maximum is a justified physical cap.

An exact retained six-hour total is [C(end)-C(start)]/10^10. It is genuinely
obtainable from recorded cumulative endpoints; the assertion that those
endpoints survive while interior records require retrieval is imposed. The raw
data do not establish separate-channel survival rates, retrieval prices, actual
arrival timestamps or repair latency. Exact counter units are a storage
precision, not proof that the physical meter has zero measurement error.

forecast issue/arrival logs. No C24 total is used at any earlier cutoff.

For each block starting s, one request returns a single stored cumulative value
C(s+j), j in{1,...,5}. With C(s) already known this is the linear observation
sum_{h=0}^{j-1}y_{s+h}=[C(s+j)-C(s)]/10^10. It is not a free individual-hour
energy oracle. Two requested boundaries split the block into three disjoint
intervals whose totals are known. A per-pair/block hard budget of two distinct
registers corresponds to10 possible two-query batches. Sharing queries across
six model comparisons or returning an entire gap in one operation is outside
this budget model. Such a restriction must remain a declared interface, not be
described as a measured production price model.

Current geometry.json includes E,c,d,W0 and source IDs. E directly reveals
held-out block totals. With the already distributed learned forecast, a full
pair coefficient involving persistence reveals the omitted persistence values.
Normalized c can still reveal shape and joint constraints across pairs; removing
E alone does not establish that the export is non-reconstructive or licensed.
Thus do not describe arbitrary per-case geometry as automatically redistribution-
safe. Strongest transparent option: distribute source code and aggregate result
summaries; recreate counter-dependent per-case geometry, LP witnesses and replay
traces from the original pinned downloads. A separate rights determination or
permission would be needed for a more permissive redistribution claim.
