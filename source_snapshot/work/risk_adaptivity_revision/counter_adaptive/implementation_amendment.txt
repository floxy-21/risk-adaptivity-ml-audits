After completing the coefficient-only geometry runs, independent review
confirmed their formulas and primary counts. Two implementation hardening
changes were applied before final reporting, with no change to thresholds,
cases, forecasts, query dictionary, budget or policy objectives:

1. Explicit raw/forecast hash gates and exact calendar/meter identity checks.
   The independent data auditor had already verified those identities.
2. Conditional second-query minimization uses exact Fraction arithmetic on
   original serialized coefficients and integer-counter answer ratios. This
   removes dependence on rounded coefficient normalization and numerical
   near-tie choices. Both pilot and continuation replay are rerun. The pilot
   preliminary answers had been seen at amendment time; the change implements
   the originally specified conditional minimization, not an outcome-tuned
   decision rule. Scientific outputs are checked for any resulting changes.

study_protocol_execution_v1.py retains the source used for the frozen
coefficient-only geometry runs; the final entry point is study.py. The
original protocol.txt is unchanged and its original hash remains recorded.
