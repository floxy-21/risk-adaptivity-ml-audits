INFORMATION-FLOW CLARIFICATION -- FINAL REVIEW, 11 OCTOBER 2026

The frozen protocol and measured outputs are retained unchanged. This note
clarifies what its separation of geometry and answer replay means in code.

Separation is per audit and concerns decision dependencies. It is not an
operating-system restriction preventing the shared loader from reading a
whole endpoint table. Preparation restores day-start persistence from permitted
prior-day endpoints. Thus a register unavailable within one audit may already
be in preparation memory as permitted lag information for a later audit.

study.py:load_initial reads only the forecast columns needed for the four
models, restores persistence, and creates each case from its forecasts and
outer counters. make_geometry passes only the normalized coefficient vector
to geometry(); neither batch selection nor the first query can inspect an
endpoint dictionary. During replay, the second adaptive query uses the original
coefficients, outer total and the first queried answer. Unqueried within-block
answers enter that audit's outcome evaluation afterwards, not its decisions.

The existing independent data_audit/check_policy_inputs.py verification checks
the permitted inputs for all 4,914 cases against their pinned source records.
The final review inspected these direct data dependencies. This clarification
does not change thresholds, policies, cases, results, protocol hashes, or the
separate post-analysis covering-witness calculation.
