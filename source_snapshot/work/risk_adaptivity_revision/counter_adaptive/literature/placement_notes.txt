Numerical nuance: study.py reports A normalized by W0, while the fragment
defines A in absolute loss-width units. State this conversion at the table.
The exact c=(0,0,1,1,0,1) example has A/W0=0.5, so the conservative numeric
guarantee flag at threshold 0.5 is false by design; use its separate proof.

Comparator naming: a 'mean-optimal randomized batch' is not a precision-optimal
batch. The dual finite-profile bound U applies to every randomized batch,
including a genuinely precision-optimal one. U<1 with A<=tau establishes the
separation even without computing the latter's exact full-domain optimum.
