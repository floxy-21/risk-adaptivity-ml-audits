\section{A Complete Empirical Precision Witness}
\label{app:complete-counter-witness}
Consider meter 1, persistence versus boosting, 1 October 2017, 06:00--12:00
UTC: the earliest qualifying case for that meter. Its measured block total is
$E=23064980899/5000000000$ kWh. Set $z=y/E$ and
$q=(c-\min_hc_h)/(\max_hc_h-\min_hc_h)$. This positive normalization
preserves widths as fractions of $W_0$ because the total is exact. The
following rational vector encodes the original serialized binary64
coefficients exactly (no displayed-decimal rounding is used):
\[
q=\frac1M\begin{pmatrix}
82036457153278624\\74339551516345942\\0\\46902318275730752\\70613562702081589\\24512796865344392
\end{pmatrix},\qquad M=82036457153278624,\qquad \sum_hz_h=1.
\]
For any batch $Q$, the normalized width is $v_Q^\top z$.
Table~\ref{tab:complete-witness-widths} supplies every $Mv_Q$.
The adverse distribution assigns probability $1/2$ each to $z=e_2$ and
$z=e_5$. Its success column is at most $1/2$ for every batch, proving
the batch upper bound at threshold $1/2$.

The adaptive witness queries boundary $3$ first and observes
$t=z_1+z_2+z_3$. It queries $5$ if $t\leq1/4$ and $2$ otherwise.
Its two conditional upper bounds, in units of $W_0$, are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 L_{3,5}(t)&=t+\frac{23711244426350837}{M}(1-t),\\
 L_{3,2}(t)&=\frac{7696905636932682}{M}t+
                    \frac{46100765836737197}{M}(1-t).
 \end{aligned}
\]
On their selected branches both are at most $1/2$ (the maximum is
approximately $0.4668$). These branches cover every $t\in[0,1]$.
The equal mixture of batches $\{3,5\}$ and $\{2,3\}$ therefore succeeds
on every feasible record with probability at least $1/2$. Together with
the adverse column above this gives $S_B^\star=1/2$ and $S_A=1$.
The certificate concerns the serialized contrast and exact-total model;
additional measurement or capacity constraints require a new calculation.
