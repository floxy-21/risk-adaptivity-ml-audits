\paragraph{From precision to a model decision.}
We also ask whether the acquired readings certify a model within
$\epsilon=0.01M_2$ of the best six-hour MSE, where $M_2$ is the training
second moment. On the external panel, adaptive and greedy querying both
certify 277 of 279 blocks, versus 263 for fixed worst-width batching.
The adaptive-minus-fixed gain is 5.02 percentage points (descriptive 95\%
calendar-block interval, 2.51--8.60); it is 0.12 points on the original
panel ($-1.34$--1.71). Greedy certifies one more strict external winner than
exact adaptation, 208 versus 207. Thus feedback can help model decisions,
while exact precision planning has no observed advantage over greedy for
the external tolerance criterion. Online Resource~1, S18 reports the
full counts and unchanged tolerance sweep.
