\section{Frozen forecasts and reproducibility scope}
\label{app:historical-design}
The historical sources are UCI ElectricityLoadDiagrams20112014,
UCI Individual Household Electric Power Consumption, and the BLEM
cumulative consumption records \citep{portugal,uci,blem}.
The original study contains 30 Portugal series (357 training and 107 test
days each), one French household (587 and 246), and three BLEM meters
(266 and 91 each), for 3,729 test meter-days. The separate additional-consumer
cohort contains 30 Portugal series and 3,210 test meter-days. It does not
expand the three-meter counter study or provide another calendar.

The counter study reuses the four models' frozen predictions on every
eligible day from 1 October through 30 December 2017. Models are persistence,
standardized ridge regression, histogram gradient boosting, and a multilayer
perceptron. Learned features and fitting use earlier observations and
chronological training data; their saved specifications, training allowances,
selection records and forecasts are unchanged. The counter policy receives
the resulting fixed contrast coefficients, not a new fit or a surrogate
distribution for unobserved test targets. The full feature equations,
hyperparameters, eligibility rules and historical fitting verification are
provided in Online Resource~1's current extended-methods source.

For BLEM, an hourly target is the integer counter difference between exact
UTC hour endpoints, converted to kWh. Both endpoints must exist, and resets,
negative increments and incomplete days are excluded, with no interpolation.
The counter replay uses the three pinned original files at repository commit
\texttt{756b2c1265f964b70f72625a95d98413cdfb78bd}; source hashes and
integer telescoping checks cover all 819 six-hour blocks. This complete-day
screen is part of the benchmark and limits conclusions about natural outages.

The historical certification grid varies three gap lengths, three mask
rules, three seed labels, two aggregate resolutions and six model pairs.
Masked readings and constructed totals are benchmark inputs; actual BLEM
outer-register differences are distinguished from manufactured totals.
All 324 original source/access/tolerance cells and the extended-consumer
results remain available, including unresolved cases and dependent repeated
seed labels. The precise archive closure is performed before interval
evaluation. Hidden references check outcomes only after the permitted
information and decisions have been fixed.

The supplement separates four execution levels: current mathematical and
bundled-output replay; reconstruction of original raw inputs; the full
counter replay from its pinned sources; and historical forecast refitting.
The current main command performs the first level and reconstructs the
editable manuscript. A separate documented counter command performs the
third without refitting. Its derived counter traces are generated locally
because raw BLEM files are not redistributed. Complete commands, input
hashes, output fields, tolerances and executed reports are indexed by claim
in \texttt{CURRENT\_RESULTS\_MAP.txt}.
