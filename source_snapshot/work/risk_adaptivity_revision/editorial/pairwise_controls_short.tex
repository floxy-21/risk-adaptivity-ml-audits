\subsection{Source protocols and the separate-pair controls}
\label{sec:counter-precision-results}
The original source contains three cumulative consumption counters
\citep{blem}, with 91 eligible days per meter. The four frozen forecasters
are daily persistence, ridge regression, boosted trees and an MLP. We audit
six-hour blocks ending at 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 UTC using block endpoints
and day-start forecasts. Next-day persistence copies are not yet available
under this declared schedule; production arrival timestamps are not measured.
The interface permits five internal hourly counters. A two-reading budget
per separate-pair audit gives 4,914 tasks; a two-reading budget shared by
all pairs gives the 819-block study above. These are different resource
requirements. Full source and fitting specifications are in Online
Resource~1, S3; S6 retains the complete separate-pair controls.

In the separate-pair experiment, a fixed batch guarantees half width on
4,371 tasks, adaptation is required for the deterministic guarantee on 290,
and 253 have no such deterministic two-query guarantee. All 290 have exact
randomized-batch precision optimum $1/2$. Recorded adaptive success is
98.64\%, versus 98.23\% for the best fixed worst-width batch and 97.96\% for
greedy; the adaptive--fixed difference is only 0.407 percentage points.
The prespecified 10\% feedback-prevalence target was not met (5.90\%).
Adding an illustrative hourly cap of $E/2$ leaves only eight of the 290
cases requiring feedback at the uncapped half-width threshold; an $E/3$
cap leaves none. Recorded profiles violate those caps in eight and 57
cases, respectively. S6 retains both the original-threshold and
new-constrained-width calculations, all compatibility flags and witnesses.

\subsection{External source and bounded-error controls}
\label{sec:external-hpm}
The HeatpumpMonitor source protocol \citep{hpmabout,hpmapi} selected the
first three ascending system IDs with public MID status, explicit Class-1
electrical-meter metadata and a cumulative feed spanning January--July
2025. Systems 1, 2 and 7 qualified. The source rule preceded hourly
retrieval. Training gates, set after inspecting training data but before
July retrieval, required 95\% valid hourly intervals and 90 complete days.
All three passed without replacement. January--June supplied training;
July supplied 31 test days per system. Each of the four model specifications
was fitted once, without retuning. The source, exclusion and fitting
chronology is retained in S6. The later shared-query analysis reuses these
sources and is retrospective.

Targets are increments of stored API-grid cumulative samples. The interface
may label a selected native sample with a grid timestamp \citep{hpmcode};
native acquisition and historical delivery times are unavailable. The
stored-record interpretation follows Table~\ref{tab:measurement-assumptions}.
Class-1 metadata is an eligibility condition, not a pointwise error bound.

All 1,674 separate-pair tasks (279 shared blocks) remain eligible. A fixed
batch guarantees half width on 1,614, feedback supplies the deterministic
guarantee on 29, and 31 have no such two-query guarantee. The 29 span every
system and model pair and have exact batch precision optimum $1/2$.
The adaptive--fixed recorded gain over all tasks is only 0.119 percentage
points; greedy succeeds on all 29 selected cases. S6 retains the complete
comparisons and exact checks of 16,740 terminal intervals.

\paragraph{Error allowances and required budgets.}
\label{sec:stored-error-sensitivity}
With exact endpoints and fixed internal-readout errors bounded by
$0.001E$ or $0.01E$, respectively 27 and 22 of those 29 cases retain the
two-query adaptive guarantee. Both levels cover every system and model
pair. Exact lower witnesses rule out a deterministic two-query guarantee
for the remaining cases; no randomized-adaptive impossibility is claimed.
A separate certified rule uses two adaptive readings for 22 cases and a
fixed three-reading batch for seven at $0.01E$, guaranteeing precision in
all 29. Its 65 reserved readings compare with 87 for fixed batches, but
require 51 information rounds rather than 29. This is a post-analysis
per-pair hard-budget result, not expected stopping cost or a shared-query
result. S6 provides the complete latent-error model, rational branch and
lower witnesses, and three-reading certificates. The next section tests
the provider's actual unrestricted interface.
