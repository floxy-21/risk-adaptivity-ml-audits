\paragraph{Replay of the precision-optimal batch.}
The randomized batch that optimizes worst-record precision has expected
success over its frozen mixture of 54.15\% on original and 71.83\% on external
recorded blocks, compared with 78.27\% and 90.32\% for adaptation.
The exact certificates establish the
worst-case separation; these replay differences compare specified policies
on the six observed systems. Online Resource~1, S18 gives the complete
