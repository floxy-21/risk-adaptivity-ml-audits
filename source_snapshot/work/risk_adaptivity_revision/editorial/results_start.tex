\section{Results}\label{sec:evaluation}
We report precision guarantees, recorded outcomes and the reading budgets
needed to meet the half-width target. All methods use the same initial
information and permitted queries.
The exact-reading panel divides into three acquisition decisions:
\[
1{,}098=
\underbrace{290}_{\text{fixed two suffice}}+
\underbrace{225}_{\text{feedback saves one}}+
\underbrace{583}_{\text{more than two required}}.
\]
Within the 225 reading-saving blocks, 191 isolate the joint-only mechanism.
Of the 222 matched-half certificates, 179 remain valid throughout
$[0.49,0.51]$. The following analyses use these fixed subsets.
