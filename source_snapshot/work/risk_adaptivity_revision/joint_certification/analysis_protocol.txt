SHARED-BUDGET VALIDATION PLAN -- 13 October 2026

Status: a new analysis of previously used data, not an untouched replication
or preregistered study. This file fixes the sensitivity reporting scope before
its cap/error results are incorporated into the manuscript. The earlier
uncapped/noiseless feasibility results were already inspected.

Question: Can one two-reading acquisition policy narrow all six comparisons
from four frozen forecasters on the same six-hour block?

Keep every eligible original block (819) and external block (279), with all
six positive-initial-width contrasts. Do not choose pairs or blocks based on
the new outcome. Positive initial widths and the fixed denominator are
checked. Normalize each contrast by its original uncapped, exact-total
initial width. The primary precision requirement is max_r W_r/W_r0 <= 1/2.
Do not redefine this denominator after adding a cap or error allowance.

Every selective policy receives two distinct internal cumulative readings
total, reused across every comparison. A batch receives them in one round;
an adaptive policy has two rounds. Bulk supplies all five optional readings
and is a separate service alternative when allowed. Counting readings is
not counting HTTP requests or money. The prior measured bulk advantage stays.

Uncapped exact calculations: deterministic joint worst-width batch, optimal
randomized batch for mean joint width, uniform batch, greedy adaptive, exact
deterministic adaptive two-query rule. Minimax mean risk is E[max_r width],
not max_r E[width]. The convex simplex vertex argument applies to this mean
objective, not to a precision-failure indicator. Compare deterministic
adaptive risk with the randomized-batch lower certificate to identify
instances with a provable numerical separation. Do not label deterministic
adaptive solutions globally optimal among randomized adaptive policies.

Selection uses initial coefficient geometry only. Save all plans before
opening fine-resolution responses in each run. Resolve primary and secondary
numeric ties within 1e-12, then use the earliest index. Randomized LP optimal
face refinement uses uniform vertex mean, independent of recorded answers.
An oracle over that face may be reported only as an explicitly retrospective
tie diagnostic, never as a deployable baseline or a new selection rule.

Sensitivity: test hourly upper bounds E/2 and E/3 at the unchanged threshold.
Record any observed trace violating the assumed cap as incompatible, rather
than quietly removing it. Recompute sharp capped intervals and validate
new planning formulas by independently assembled endpoint LPs. Do not reuse
the uncapped vertex-only exactness assertion for capped joint mean risk.

Stored-reading sensitivity: exact endpoints, one fixed latent internal error
vector with each error bounded by 0.01 E. Validate the unchanged frozen
two-query policies over every possible permitted answer, using branchwise
two-completion LPs. Compare with the best deterministic batch under the same
error model. The external 279-block panel is the primary error-validation
panel; any added original-panel run must be separately identified. A fixed
policy's valid upper certificate is not an optimal noisy-adaptive theorem.
Nominal-response replay uses the actual stored readings and enlarged
compatible fibers; it is not a device calibration or a noise simulation.

Report all favorable and unfavorable outcomes, per source and meter, mean
terminal joint width, recorded precision, guarantee coverage, and numerical
residuals. Preserve capacity/error qualifications and small greedy gains.
No target effect size or acceptance probability is a pass/fail criterion.

Reproducibility: original BLEM raw traces and counter-dependent geometry
retain the established raw-regeneration route and are not redistributed.
Public external caches can support offline replay. Mathematical constructed
witnesses and derived aggregate result ledgers are separate from raw data.
