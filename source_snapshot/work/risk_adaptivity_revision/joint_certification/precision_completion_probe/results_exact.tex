\paragraph{Exact comparison with randomized batching.}
We solved the half-width precision game on every block.

feasible adverse distributions and rational branch covers close all

guarantees success, whereas the best randomized batch has worst-record
success $1/2$ on 222 blocks and $1/3$ on the remaining three.
The comparison therefore includes every randomized mixture of the ten
