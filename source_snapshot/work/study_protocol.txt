Question: Under explicitly simulated missing verification targets, do preserved
energy totals identify which of two fixed forecasts has lower daily MSE?
Data: UCI ElectricityLoadDiagrams20112014 (Portugal client panel) and UCI
Individual Household Electric Power Consumption (one French household).
Neither provides independent backup registers for this experiment. Totals will
be derived from complete retained truth BEFORE masking; this emulates aggregate
survival and is NOT a field outage study. No forecast improvement is claimed.

Portugal: original 15-minute end-of-interval kW readings, shift index back 15 min,
hourly interval energy=sum/4. Train 2013; use first 30 meter IDs with >=99% finite
training hours and >95% positive training hours. Test first 28 days of Jan, Apr,
Jul, Oct 2014. Exclude the provider's DST transition days from evaluation.
Household: minute average global active power, hourly kWh=sum/60; require all 60
finite minutes. Train 2008-2009; test all available complete eligible days 2010.
Only days with all 24 target values and all origin-available features are used.
Final counts are reported after these prespecified completeness filters.

Forecasts: previous-day same hour, previous-week same hour, StandardScaler+Ridge
(alpha=1), HistGradientBoostingRegressor (100 iterations, learning_rate=.08,
max_leaf_nodes=15,min_samples_leaf=20,l2_regularization=1,max_bins=127,seed=1729).
No outcome-based hyperparameter search. Fixed features per target hour: lag24,
lag48,lag168,last24hmean and sine/cosine of hour,weekday,month. At each local-grid
00:00 origin, all 24 values of each lag are available; last24hmean uses yesterday.
Hourly feature model shares parameters across horizons. Predictions clipped >=0.
All forecasts are generated and frozen before target masking.

Verification masks: exactly 2,6,12 targets per 24h day; (i) contiguous random gap,
(ii) random scattered gap, (iii) highest-demand targets (oracle adversarial stress,
not an empirical missingness mechanism). Three fixed seeds 1103,2207,3301 for
random mechanisms; highest-demand repeated across seeds for a balanced design,

Total-error sensitivity: symmetric 0%,1%,5% of each complete block's total.
Feasible targets nonnegative with no upper capacity or smoothness constraints.

Compare all 6 unordered model pairs, per day. Numerical decision margin
1e-9*max(1,abs(bound endpoints)); indeterminate if interval includes margin.
Methods: direct sharp paired bounds; separate sharp individual SSE bounds
combined into an independent envelope; complete-case comparison; uniform
energy-preserving imputation; training weekday/hour-profile energy-preserving

Energy-constrained baselines use central aggregate totals; uncertainty sensitivity
