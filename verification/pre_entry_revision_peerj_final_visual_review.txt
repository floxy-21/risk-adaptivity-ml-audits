2. STRICT CHECK OF THE NEW MATHEMATICS
The initial widths are exactly 2H because the target core and independent
z box each contribute range H. After querying t and z_j, the exact widths
are H+mt-a_j and H+m(1-t)-b_j. The declared branch partition covers all
t in [0,1], including endpoint ties, and guarantees both widths <=H-1/4.

The uniform nonadaptive mixture over {t,z_j} has success >=1/m for every
feasible record. This is a continuum lower bound, not a finite-grid estimate.
At x_i=((i-1/2)/m,0,...,0), {t,z_j} has largest width H+|i-j|-3/4 and
succeeds exactly if i=j. The exact table also covers {t,u}, {t,v}, {u,v},
{u,z_j}, {v,z_j}, and {z_j,z_k}; all fail on all adverse records.
The last type fails because its two removed coefficient sums total
2m+3<2H. Equal adverse masses therefore bound every batch mixture by 1/m.
Shorter, repeated and stopped nonadaptive query plans cannot improve upon
their two-distinct-query extensions. The adversary does not see the seed.

For T3=T1-T2, P=floor(m^2/4), the exact initial width is 2(H+P) and the
width after t alone is m+2P. The displayed inequality establishes the
three-pair guarantee throughout the normalized half-open threshold band.
The band has radius 1/(8H)=1/[2m(2m+3)]; its lower endpoint is included
and upper endpoint excluded. The common forecast realization has the
required coefficient vectors up to additive constants and pair orientation.

The fixed batch {t,z_1,z_m} succeeds with three readings. Consequently
the paper correctly states a growing probability gap at budget two, not
unbounded savings in the number of queries. The shrinking normalized margin
does not establish robustness to a fixed measurement-error percentage.

The new result does not contradict the scalar Helly theorem: one scalar
conditional width is concave and has convex strict failure sets. The joint
maximum has potentially disconnected failure sets. The manuscript preserves
this exact distinction and attributes established sparse-policy reasoning.
