\paragraph{Replay against the precision-optimal challenger.}
The frozen precision-optimal batch policy succeeds on 54.15\% of original
blocks and 71.83\% of external blocks in expectation, compared with
78.27\% and 90.32\% for adaptation (Table~\ref{tab:complete-precision-replay}).
The adaptive advantage is positive in each of the six systems.
On the matched-half blocks, adaptation succeeds on every recorded block;
the batch policy succeeds on 65.75\% and 65.08\% in expectation.
This challenger optimizes worst-record precision success; the replay
measures success on the recorded trace. The gains over deterministic
worst-width batching are 7.81 and 11.11 percentage points, and those over
greedy adaptation are 2.20 and 2.15 points. These comparisons answer
different questions: the exact certificates establish the assurance
separation, while the replay compares the specified policies on these six
systems.
